Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2023

Welcome to QR Scanner For All. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Data Collection and Usage

QR Scanner For All is a privacy-first application. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal data, analytics, or usage logs to our servers or any third-party servers.

  • Scan History & Created QR Codes: All your scanned QR codes, generated QR codes, and application settings are stored locally on your device. We have no access to this data.

2. Permissions We Request

To provide its core functionality, the App requires the following device permissions:

  • Camera: Required to scan QR codes and barcodes in real-time. The image stream is processed entirely on your device. No images or videos are ever recorded, saved, or uploaded to the internet.
  • Vibrate (Haptic Feedback): Used to provide a physical response (vibration) when a QR code is successfully scanned.

3. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes Google ML Kit (an on-device machine learning tool) for high-performance barcode and QR code recognition. This process happens locally on your device and does not send image data to Google's servers.

4. Children's Privacy

Our App is safe for children. Since we do not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
